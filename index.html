Vision & Mission

Why KEB exists

Vision
A region where growing businesses run on one connected ecosystem — software shaped around how they actually work, and marketing shaped around who they actually serve — instead of a patchwork of disconnected tools and vendors.
Mission
We build the CRM systems that run a business and the campaigns that grow it as one connected practice, so every client gets an ecosystem designed to work together from day one — not two vendors bolted together after the fact.

Two disciplines, one team

A new studio, built to give every early client full attention

In practice, that means direct access to the people doing the work — not a layer of account managers — and a company motivated to prove itself on results, not on a slide of past clients.
